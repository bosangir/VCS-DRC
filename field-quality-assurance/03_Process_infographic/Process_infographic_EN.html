Quality data,a PSU covered in full

The path of one record, from departure to the server. Each step follows the Sankuru protocol (SOP-01 and DQA Plan V1.0) and leaves a trace that can be checked: a GPS point, a time, a submitted record.

Every structure counts.
Every record can be checked.
1
BEFORE AND ON ARRIVAL

Prepare the evening before

  • PSU and segment polygons visible on the CAPI map; Segment 1 written on the assignment sheet.
  • CAPI on the right version, a test submission sent; phone and power bank charged.
  • Consent forms in French and Otetela; sensitisation confirmed with the local authority.
Who: Team chief, enumerator
2
BEFORE AND ON ARRIVAL

Arrive at the PSU

  • The centroid is for navigation: nobody is interviewed on the way or at the centroid.
  • The blue dot must be inside the PSU polygon. If not, do not start.
  • Courtesy visit to the local authority before the first household.
Who: Enumerator, team chief
3
BEFORE AND ON ARRIVAL

Start in Segment 1

  • BGC numbered the segments at random: Segment 1 is a random start. Nobody chooses it.
  • Get inside the Segment 1 polygon before the first structure.
  • Fix a serpentine sweep direction and tell the team chief.
Who: Enumerator
4
INSIDE THE SEGMENT

Every structure, one record

  • Start with the structure nearest the entry point, then the next one on the line.
  • Skip nothing: far away, uphill, fenced or "no child here".
  • Vacant, non-residential or abandoned area: a record with GPS anyway. It proves the area was checked.
Who: Enumerator
5
INSIDE THE SEGMENT

The same sequence, everywhere

  • Caregiver recall first, then the card. Enter every visible date.
  • A date prompt? Recheck against the card before leaving. If the card shows that date, keep it and note it in the comment.
  • Clear photo of the whole card; GPS ≤ 15 m at the door; record closed and submitted on the spot.
Who: Enumerator
6
INSIDE THE SEGMENT

Absent, refusing, inaccessible

  • Absent: record, then a revisit the same day or next morning. At most two revisits, then a documented non-contact.
  • Refusal: record with no personal data. Never replaced by the neighbour.
  • Inaccessible or unsafe: reason code; team chief and supervisor told the same day.
Who: Enumerator, supervisor
7
CLOSING THE PSU

Always finish the segment

  • Quota reached halfway through the segment? Finish the segment anyway (Rule 5.1).
  • Declare the segment to the team chief: records, eligible households, interviews, absent households to revisit.
  • Next segment = next number, found on the map. It is not always the neighbour.
Who: Enumerator, team chief
8
CLOSING THE PSU

Close the PSU

  • Return to absent households before declaring the PSU complete.
  • A code A to H for every segment from 1 to the highest reached. The code must match the GPS points on the map.
  • Skipping a segment: the field supervisor alone decides. Replacing a PSU: DQM and PI, backup PSU from the BGC list.
Who: Team chief, supervisor
9
THE SAME DAY AND AFTER

Same-day review

  • Team chief: first review of every record (complete, photo, GPS, segment, PSU).
  • Field supervisor: second review, entries compared with the photo, then upload.
  • Data management, then data quality: alerts A1–A13, queries to the teams, corrected the same day.
Who: The whole chain
10
THE SAME DAY AND AFTER

Independent checks

  • Back-checks on at least 10% of interviews (5% random, 5% targeted), by phone within 48 hours.
  • Coverage audit: GPS points on satellite imagery; re-sweep of segments where structures are missing.
  • 100% of cards reviewed with the HBR tool; direct observation of 5–10% of interviews.
Who: Independent back-checkers, DQM

Skipping a structure

13 & 16 NOV 2026

New after the BGC review

  • A record for empty or abandoned areas too, with GPS.
  • The disposition code must match the map: no "A" while structures are left without a record.
  • A form left open to come back later is allowed. The active interview time is recorded.
  • Doses in one series with the same or reversed dates, or same-age doses on different dates: recheck against the card.
  • "Date illegible" or "tick only": only when true. The rate is tracked by enumerator.
  • Detailed codes for inaccessible segments (forest, water, insecurity…).

Who does what

EnumeratorFollows the sequence at every structure. One record per structure, submitted on the spot.
Team chief and supervisorReview every record the same day. Only the field supervisor authorises an order change or a skipped segment.
Field coordinatorOrganises teams, return visits and re-interviews; passes on every PSU replacement.
Data managementMaintains the CAPI, maps and dashboard; produces the alerts; confirms uploads every day.
Data qualityReviews alerts, decides corrective actions, commissions back-checks and the coverage audit.
